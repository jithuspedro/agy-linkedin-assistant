Privacy Policy

AGY LinkedIn Assistant

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. About this application

AGY LinkedIn Assistant is a personal tool intended to help its owner draft LinkedIn posts and, when properly authorized, publish posts to the owner's LinkedIn account. This policy describes the intended handling of information by this application. The owner must update it if the actual implementation or data practices differ.

2. Information processed

Depending on the features enabled, the application may process:

  • Post text and instructions entered by the user.
  • Basic LinkedIn profile information returned during authorized sign-in.
  • OAuth access tokens or other authorization data needed to call LinkedIn APIs.
  • Post identifiers or API responses needed to confirm whether a post was published.

The application should request only the information and permissions needed for its stated functions.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate the account, prepare post drafts, and publish content only when the user has authorized the relevant action. Information is not intended to be used for advertising or sold.

4. Storage, security, and sharing

Any credentials and tokens must be stored securely and must not be committed to a public repository, included in prompts, or shared with unauthorized people. Data may be processed by LinkedIn and by infrastructure or AI providers that the owner chooses to use. Their own terms and privacy policies may also apply. The owner should disclose any such providers and actual data flows here before using the application with real account data.

5. Retention and deletion

The application should retain information only for as long as needed to provide its features. The user can stop further access by revoking the application's authorization through LinkedIn account settings. Locally stored tokens and application data should also be deleted when no longer needed.

6. Third-party services

LinkedIn handles account data and published content under its own terms and privacy policy. Use of LinkedIn APIs is subject to LinkedIn's applicable developer terms and permissions. LinkedIn Privacy Policy.

7. Children's privacy

This application is not intended to be directed at children and should not knowingly collect children's personal information.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change. The latest version should be published at this URL.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the contact details provided on the associated LinkedIn developer application or repository.
